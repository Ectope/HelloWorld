Educational Information

NBG Pacemaker Code

Position I: Chamber paced (A=Atrial, V=Ventricular, D=Dual)

Position II: Chamber sensed (A, V, D, O=None)

Position III: Response to sensing (I=Inhibited, T=Triggered, D=Dual)

Common Modes

  • VVI: Paces ventricle, senses ventricle, inhibited by sensed beat
  • AAI: Paces atrium, senses atrium, inhibited by sensed beat
  • DDD: Paces both, senses both, dual response
  • VOO/AOO: Asynchronous - no sensing, fixed rate

Failure to Capture

Pacing spike present but no myocardial response. Causes: lead displacement, increased threshold, low output, lead fracture.

Fix: Increase output, check lead position, check connections.

Failure to Sense

Pacemaker doesn't detect intrinsic activity. Paces inappropriately.

Fix: Increase sensitivity (lower mV number), check lead position.

Oversensing

Pacemaker detects non-cardiac signals. Inappropriate inhibition.

Fix: Decrease sensitivity (higher mV number), check for EMI.

Capture Threshold

Minimum output (mA) required to depolarize the myocardium. Typically set output at 2-3x threshold for safety margin.

Sensitivity

Ability to detect intrinsic cardiac activity. Lower mV = more sensitive. Higher mV = less sensitive.

AV Delay

Time between atrial and ventricular pacing in DDD mode. Normal PR interval is 120-200ms.
